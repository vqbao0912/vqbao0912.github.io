01 / ABOUT

Research grounded
in real applications.

I am a lecturer in the School of Electrical Engineering at International University, Vietnam National University Ho Chi Minh City.

My research connects the foundations of telecommunications with applications in intelligent systems. I investigate full-duplex communications, channel coding, and physical-layer security, alongside computer vision and machine learning for biomedical imaging and practical sensing systems.

I received my Ph.D. in Telecommunications from the University of Brest, France, in 2022. At International University, I also support student activities and supervise undergraduate research.
